# Conclusion 学术句式库（深度学习论文）

> 高频动词（`we + V`）：`have presented / proposed / introduced` / `show` / `demonstrate` / `find` / `observe` / `believe` / `hope` / `plan` / `leave` / `explore`
> 原则：简洁总结（不照抄摘要，不引入新实验结果），坦诚说明局限性与未来方向。

## 1. 总结工作（现在完成时 / 一般过去时）
- *In this paper, we have presented **[Name]**, a [concise summary] designed to address [problem].*
- *We introduced [key idea] and showed how it enables [key capability] without [previous cost].*
- *Across [N] benchmarks, our experiments demonstrate that **[Name]** achieves strong gains in both [metric A] and [metric B].*

## 2. 局限性（顶会审稿人看重坦诚与具体）
- *Our approach has several limitations.*
- *First, **[Name]** currently assumes [assumption], which may not hold in [edge case].*
- *Second, although [aspect] is efficient, [other aspect] still incurs non-trivial overhead at [scale].*
- *While our evaluation covers [domains], its behavior under [untested shift] remains to be studied.*

## 3. 未来工作与展望
- *Extending **[Name]** to [broader setting / modality] is a promising direction for future work.*
- *In future work, we plan to investigate [specific extension] and explore how [idea] interacts with [related paradigm].*
- *We leave [open problem] as an interesting avenue for future research.*
- *We hope our findings / framework will facilitate further research on [topic].*
