# Related Work 学术句式库（深度学习论文）

> 高频动词：`propose` / `focus` / `consider` / `introduce` / `review` / `extend` / `address` / `apply` / `explore`
> 核心原则：**不是流水账罗列文献**，而是按路线归类，并在**每段末尾明确点出与本文的区别**。

## 1. 路线归类与综述开篇
- *Existing approaches to [task] can be broadly categorized into two / three lines of work: [A] and [B].*
- *Prior work on [topic] largely falls into two paradigms: ...*
- *A growing body of literature has investigated [topic] [CITE].*
- *Another line of work focuses on [direction] [CITE].*

## 2. 归纳代表工作（避免每句都 *X et al. proposed...*）
- *Early studies [CITE] formulated [task] as ..., whereas more recent approaches [CITE] leverage ...*
- *[Author] et al. [CITE] introduce [concept] to [goal]. Building on this, [Author] et al. [CITE] further extend ...*
- *Several methods [CITE] aim to improve [property] by [mechanism].*
- *To reduce [cost], [CITE] replace [heavy op] with [lightweight op].*

## 3. 指出现有路线共性局限
- *While these methods perform well under [setting], they struggle when [condition].*
- *However, most of these approaches rely on [strong supervision / heavy compute / assumption].*
- *Although [CITE] addresses [issue A], it does not consider [issue B].*

## 4. 点明与本文的区别（每段必有）
- *In contrast to the above methods, our work [key difference].*
- *Different from [CITE], which [their approach], we [our approach] without requiring [extra constraint].*
- *Our work is most closely related to [CITE], but differs in two key aspects: first, ...; second, ...*
- *Rather than [existing design], we focus on [our focus], making our approach complementary to [CITE].*
